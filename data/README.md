# data/

This folder is intentionally empty. The 2022 Ghana Demographic and Health Survey files are not included, because the DHS terms do not allow redistribution of the data or of datasets derived from them.

To reproduce the analysis, request the 2022 Ghana Births Recode (`GHBR8CFL`) from the DHS Program at https://dhsprogram.com/data/ and follow the steps in the main README.
